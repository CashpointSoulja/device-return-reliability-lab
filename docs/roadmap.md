# My v2 roadmap

Ayo Ahmed - Independent concept

**Unofficial concept. Synthetic data. No live integrations.**

I would advance each stage only after discovery supports its value and the preceding integrity controls survive a bounded pilot.

1. **Validate the slice.** Observe operations and supplier dispatch, agree SLA/ownership definitions, sample exception traces, and test whether the primary bottleneck is capacity, evidence, communication or inventory demand.
2. **Harden event reconciliation.** Add authenticated supplier identities, reservation expiry, cancellation cutoffs, replay windows, reconciliation queues and regional capacity models. Preserve append-only audit provenance and operator review for ambiguous events.
3. **Integrate authoritative proof.** Define warehouse scan/condition standards and security attestation provenance. Add governed evidence storage, legal hold and repair release policy. Keep failed prerequisites explicit.
4. **Pilot bounded automation.** Connect one supplier/region, approved message channels and explicit support rules. Measure readiness and intervention denominators with false-positive/unsafe-readiness guardrails.
5. **Expand supplier self-service.** Add capability onboarding, regional SLA policies, tracking adapters and labels after reconciliation and permissions are validated. Broaden the platform from proven need, not demo breadth.

I would not add a predictive SLA score before testing rule usefulness, data completeness, bias by region/supplier and the operational cost of false alerts. I would not claim reduced cost or staff workload until measured against an agreed cohort and baseline.
